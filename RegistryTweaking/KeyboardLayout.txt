A fix for extra keyboard layout reappearing. The problem is that I have configured US and SWE keyboards,
but every now and then a UK keyboard gets autmatically added to the list.

In the registry, under:
HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\Control\ContentIndex\Language

There is a subkey for each language. Remove the whole sub key for the unwanted language (UK in this case).
Log out and in again.
